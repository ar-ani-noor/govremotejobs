GDIT — General Dynamics Information Technology, a business unit of General Dynamics — is a major federal IT contractor and a consistent source of remote positions we track, particularly for technology-focused roles.

## What GDIT does

GDIT delivers IT services, systems integration, cybersecurity, cloud infrastructure, and mission support work across defense, intelligence, federal civilian, and health agency customers. It's one of the largest federal IT contractors by revenue and headcount, which translates into a broad range of open positions across many technical disciplines at any given time.

## Why GDIT offers meaningful remote opportunity

As with other major federal contractors, GDIT operates under normal private-sector employment terms rather than the federal workforce policies that sharply cut remote work for direct federal employees — see our guide on [why remote federal jobs became rare](/guides/why-remote-federal-jobs-became-rare). GDIT's scale and technology focus (see our [IT and cybersecurity guide](/guides/it-and-cybersecurity-remote-government-jobs)) means a meaningful share of its open roles are desk-based technical work well suited to remote arrangements.

## What roles typically show up

GDIT's remote postings skew heavily technical: software development, cloud engineering, cybersecurity and information assurance, systems administration, data engineering and analytics, and IT program management, among others. Given GDIT's breadth of federal customers, the specific mission focus of a role (defense, health, civilian agencies) varies widely — read the position description for context on which customer and program a specific opening supports.

## Security clearances

Clearance requirements at GDIT vary by contract and customer — some roles, particularly those supporting federal civilian or health agencies, require no clearance at all, while defense- and intelligence-focused roles often do. Check each posting individually rather than assuming a uniform requirement.

## How applying works

GDIT's hiring process follows standard private-sector conventions — apply through their careers site, expect a timeline measured in weeks rather than the months typical of direct federal hiring, and use a normal professional resume rather than the detailed federal format.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does GDIT sponsor security clearances for candidates who don't already have one?** It depends on the specific role and contract — some positions state they'll sponsor a clearance process for the right candidate, while others require an active, transferable clearance as a hard prerequisite because contract staffing timelines don't allow for a lengthy investigation. Read each posting's clearance language carefully rather than assuming either way.

**How is GDIT different from its parent company, General Dynamics?** General Dynamics is a large, diversified defense and aerospace company; GDIT is specifically its information technology services business unit, focused on IT, cybersecurity, and digital transformation work for government customers — distinct from General Dynamics' other business areas like combat vehicles or shipbuilding.

**Does GDIT hire outside of IT?** Yes, though IT and technology roles dominate their postings — program management, business analysis, and various specialized support roles tied to specific contracts also appear regularly. Browse broadly rather than filtering only on technical titles.
